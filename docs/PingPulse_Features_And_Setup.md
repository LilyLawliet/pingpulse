# Everything PingPulse does

PingPulse answers your customers on WhatsApp, around the clock, using your own
prices and your own rules. It books appointments into your calendar, takes
orders from your price list, and hands the conversation to you the moment it
should.

It is not a chatbot with a script. It reads the documents you upload and
answers from those — and where it has nothing to go on, it says so and fetches
you, rather than inventing an answer.

---

## 1. Answering customers

- **Day and night, in their language.** Customers write in whatever language
  they use; the agent replies in the same one.
- **Prices come from your documents only.** Upload a price list, catalogue or
  brochure — PDF, Word, Excel or photographs — and it quotes from that. It is
  forbidden from inventing a figure. If something is not in there, it says it
  will check.
- **It answers each message once**, however many times WhatsApp delivers it.
- **It sounds like you, if you want it to.** Give it past replies you have
  written and it drafts a description of your voice — and shows you that
  description before anything changes.
- **One follow-up, once**, if somebody goes quiet. You choose whether it is
  sent at all, and nobody is messaged at three in the morning.

## 2. Saying yes, no, or "I'll get someone"

Asked whether you do something, there are three answers and no fourth:

- **Yes** — it is on your list, and the conversation moves toward booking it.
- **No** — it is not something you do. It says so plainly and names what you
  *do* do, so the customer is not left guessing.
- **A person** — asked for a human, it stops and tells you.

It will not say "I'm not sure", "I think so", or "that might be possible".
Those are not answers, and the software does not allow them.

A "no" is remembered. If a customer asks for work you do not do, gives their
details and then says *yes*, nothing is booked — because nothing was ever
offered. The refusal lifts only when they ask for work you actually do.

## 3. Booking appointments

- **Only times you can honour.** Inside your opening hours, in your timezone,
  and free in your calendar. A time the customer suggests is checked the same
  way.
- **It reads the booking back before writing it** — the job, the date, the
  time and the address — and books only after the customer confirms.
- **It names the work.** "Site visit for kitchen remodeling", not just
  "appointment", so nobody turns up to the wrong job.
- **A question about a time is never taken as a booking.**
- **Nothing is called booked unless it is.** The confirmation is written from
  the calendar entry itself, never from what the agent intended to do.

## 4. Checking contact details

Before anything is booked, the phone number and email address are checked.

An unusable number or a malformed email is refused in plain words — *"the
email address "ali@" is not a valid email address… nothing is booked until I
have one that works"* — and nothing is recorded. A correct one is kept and
used.

This happens in the software, not in the AI, so it holds even when the AI is
slow or unavailable.

## 5. Taking orders

A customer orders in the chat and the agent does the rest:

1. **What they want** — matched to your price list only. A choice like "pink
   dotted or mint lined" is asked, never guessed.
2. **Where it goes** — if delivery cost depends on the city, it asks the city,
   then the full address.
3. **How they pay** — only the methods your documents name (cash on delivery,
   bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa…), in your own wording.
4. **The summary** — items, delivery, total, address and payment, worked out
   from your price list.
5. **The yes** — only the customer's confirmation places it. They get an order
   number and your delivery wording; you get a notification.

## 6. Where you watch it

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Every conversation live. The agent's replies are marked as the agent's, yours as yours. Take over any conversation at any time — it stops replying to that person immediately and keeps everyone else running. |
| **Board** | Every customer, at the stage they have reached, in your own words. |
| **Orders** | What was ordered, by whom, and what it is worth. |
| **Calendar** | Everything booked, and your availability. Appointments also reach your phone's calendar. |
| **Analytics** | How it is going: how many got as far as qualified, where people stop replying, how fast they are answered and by whom. Only leads with a real figure against them are counted in the money, and the screen says how many that is. |
| **Test agent** | Type any message and see exactly what the agent would say — the real agent, your real prices, with nothing sent to anyone. |

## 7. Being told when you are needed

Alerts reach you by email or on your phone when somebody asks for a person,
when a lead is worth your attention, or when something breaks. If your
WhatsApp phone goes offline, a red warning appears until it reconnects —
nothing locks, and nothing is silently dropped.

## 8. Where it runs

One product, three ways in, all showing the same thing:

- **The desktop app**, which updates itself when you open it.
- **Any web browser**, at your dashboard address.
- **Your phone's browser**, for checking in.

# What you need to do

Setting up takes about twenty minutes, and most of it is finding files you
already have. The **Setup** screen lists ten steps in three groups and ticks
them off as you go — and the ticks are read from the real system, so a step
cannot be marked done just by visiting it.

The steps can be done in any order. Start with whatever you have to hand.

## Required — it cannot start without these

| # | Step | What to have ready |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your business** | Your business name, what you sell, and your currency. |
| 2 | **Prices and knowledge** | Your price list, catalogue or brochure. PDF, Word, Excel or photographs all work. This is what stops it inventing a price. |
| 3 | **Where you are** | Your timezone, and the areas you cover. Every hour you state and every appointment offered is read in your zone. |
| 4 | **Connect WhatsApp** | The phone that owns the number. You scan a QR code with it, or connect your own Twilio account. |

Until all four are done, the inbox, board and analytics stay locked and
PingPulse tells you which steps are still open. **Test agent** opens as soon
as your business and prices are in, so you can try it before any customer can
reach it.

WhatsApp is deliberately last: the moment it connects, real customers start
getting answers.

## Recommended — it runs without these, but something is missing

| # | Step | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Hours and booking** | Without your opening hours the agent cannot book anything — every booking request is handed to a person instead. |
| 6 | **Alerts** | An email address or phone number, so being needed reaches a person instead of going nowhere. |
| 7 | **Calendar** | So appointments appear on the calendar you already use. |

## Optional — fine to leave as they are

| # | Step | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Your board** | Rename the stages to the words your team actually uses. |
| 9 | **Learning** | Past replies you have written, so it sounds like you. |
| 10 | **Problems** | Things you want flagged to a person rather than answered. |

## Then: try it before anyone else does

Open **Test agent** and ask it what your most awkward customer would ask. It
is the real agent with your real prices; nothing is sent to anyone. If an
answer is wrong, the fix is almost always a document — add it under *Prices
and knowledge* and ask again.

# The rules it will not break

These are enforced in the software. They are not instructions the agent can be
talked out of.

- **It never says something happened unless it did.** No confirmed booking, no
  promised callback, unless the system actually recorded it.
- **It never invents a price.** If it is not in what you uploaded, it says it
  will check.
- **It never promises what only you can do.** It will not say it has lowered a
  price, changed a quotation, or will send an invoice. Asked for one, it
  quotes your terms.
- **It only offers pictures you have.**
- **It never claims to be a person.** Asked directly, it says what it is.
- **It only offers times it can actually honour.**
- **It never says an appointment was booked, moved or cancelled** unless the
  calendar changed on that message.
- **It answers each message once**, and sends one follow-up once.
- **It stops when somebody asks for a person**, and tells you.
- **It never messages anyone at three in the morning.**
- **It never learns from its own replies** — only from yours.

---

# What it does not do

Said plainly, so there are no surprises:

- It does not take payments. It tells customers how you take payment, in your
  wording, but no money moves through PingPulse.
- It does not issue invoices, quotations or proformas.
- It does not change your prices. It reads what you upload.
- It does not message anyone first. It answers people who write to you.
- It does not learn from its own replies, so a mistake does not compound.

---

# If something goes wrong

- **A wrong answer** is nearly always a missing or unclear document. Add it
  under *Prices and knowledge* and check in **Test agent**.
- **WhatsApp offline** shows as a red warning. Reconnect the phone; nothing is
  lost in the meantime.
- **Anything else** — take over the conversation from the inbox. The agent
  stops replying to that person immediately and everyone else keeps running.

# PingPulse Desktop — Installation Guide

**For: PingPulse clients**
Windows 10 and 11 · About 2 minutes

---

## What you are installing

The PingPulse desktop dashboard. It shows your WhatsApp conversations as they
happen, your sales pipeline, and what the AI agent is replying to each customer —
in a proper application window rather than a browser tab.

The installer is small (under 2 MB) because the app uses the browser engine
Windows already has. There is nothing else to install.

---

## Step 1 — Run the installer

Double-click **`PingPulse_Setup.exe`**.

---

## Step 2 — If Windows shows a blue warning screen

You will most likely see a blue box titled **"Windows protected your PC"**.

**This is expected. Nothing is wrong.**

Windows shows this for any application it has not yet seen downloaded by
thousands of people. It is a reputation check, not a virus warning — our
installer is new, so it has no reputation yet.

To continue:

1. Click **More info** — the small link in the blue box
2. Click **Run anyway** — the button that then appears at the bottom

```
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  Windows protected your PC                 │
   │                                            │
   │  Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented  │
   │  an unrecognised app from starting.        │
   │                                            │
   │  More info      ←  1. click this           │
   │                                            │
   │                          [ Don't run ]     │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   after clicking "More info":

   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  App:       PingPulse_Setup.exe            │
   │  Publisher: Unknown publisher              │
   │                                            │
   │        [ Run anyway ]   [ Don't run ]      │
   │            ↑                               │
   │            2. click this                   │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

If you do not see a **Run anyway** button, your workplace may block unsigned
applications. Contact us and we will provide a signed installer.

---

## Step 3 — Finish the installation

The installer runs on its own and takes a few seconds. It installs for your
user account only, so **no administrator password is needed**.

When it finishes, PingPulse appears in your Start menu.

---

## Step 4 — Sign in

Open **PingPulse** from the Start menu and sign in with the email and password
we set up for you.

If this is your very first time and you do not have an account yet, click
**Create an account instead** on the sign-in screen and register with your work
email. Tell us once you have, and we will connect your account to your business.

---

## Everyday use

- The app connects to your PingPulse server automatically — there is nothing to
  configure.
- Conversations appear live, the moment a customer messages you.
- You can close the window at any time. The AI agent keeps answering customers
  whether the dashboard is open or not.

---

## If something is not right

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **"Windows protected your PC"** | Expected — see Step 2. Click More info → Run anyway. |
| Blank or white window | Close and reopen the app. If it persists, contact us. |
| "Cannot connect" or no conversations loading | Check your internet connection first, then contact us — your server may need attention. |
| Sign-in rejected | Check for typos and caps lock. If it still fails, we can reset your password. |
| App will not install at all | Your workplace may restrict software installs. Ask your IT team, or contact us. |

---

## Uninstalling

Windows **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → PingPulse → Uninstall**.

Removing the app does not affect your account, your conversations or the AI
agent. Everything stays safely on your PingPulse server, and you can reinstall
at any time.

---

## A note on the security warning

We want to be straight with you about this, because being told to click past a
security warning is a reasonable thing to hesitate over.

Windows SmartScreen warns about any program it has not seen many people install
before. It is judging reputation, not scanning for anything harmful. New
software always starts with no reputation.

The warning disappears once an application is code-signed with a certificate
that verifies our identity. We are in the process of obtaining one, and once it
is in place your future updates will install with no warning at all.

If you would rather wait for the signed version, that is completely fine —
just let us know.

PingPulse · WhatsApp AI Sales Agent

Everything It Does,
and What You Do

Every feature in plain language, and the twenty minutes of setup it needs from you.

Release 1.5.1  ·  9 October 2026  ·  For a new client, before the first customer message

PingPulse answers your customers on WhatsApp, around the clock, using your own prices and your own rules. It books appointments into your calendar, takes orders from your price list, and hands the conversation to you the moment it should.

It is not a chatbot with a script. It reads the documents you upload and answers from those — and where it has nothing to go on, it says so and fetches you, rather than inventing an answer.

1. Answering customers

  • Day and night, in their language. Customers write in whatever language they use; the agent replies in the same one.
  • Prices come from your documents only. Upload a price list, catalogue or brochure — PDF, Word, Excel or photographs — and it quotes from that. It is forbidden from inventing a figure. If something is not in there, it says it will check.
  • It answers each message once, however many times WhatsApp delivers it.
  • It sounds like you, if you want it to. Give it past replies you have written and it drafts a description of your voice — and shows you that description before anything changes.
  • One follow-up, once, if somebody goes quiet. You choose whether it is sent at all, and nobody is messaged at three in the morning.

2. Saying yes, no, or "I'll get someone"

Asked whether you do something, there are three answers and no fourth:

  • Yes — it is on your list, and the conversation moves toward booking it.
  • No — it is not something you do. It says so plainly and names what you do do, so the customer is not left guessing.
  • A person — asked for a human, it stops and tells you.

It will not say "I'm not sure", "I think so", or "that might be possible". Those are not answers, and the software does not allow them.

A "no" is remembered. If a customer asks for work you do not do, gives their details and then says yes, nothing is booked — because nothing was ever offered. The refusal lifts only when they ask for work you actually do.

3. Booking appointments

  • Only times you can honour. Inside your opening hours, in your timezone, and free in your calendar. A time the customer suggests is checked the same way.
  • It reads the booking back before writing it — the job, the date, the time and the address — and books only after the customer confirms.
  • It names the work. "Site visit for kitchen remodeling", not just "appointment", so nobody turns up to the wrong job.
  • A question about a time is never taken as a booking.
  • Nothing is called booked unless it is. The confirmation is written from the calendar entry itself, never from what the agent intended to do.

4. Checking contact details

Before anything is booked, the phone number and email address are checked.

An unusable number or a malformed email is refused in plain words — "the email address "ali@" is not a valid email address… nothing is booked until I have one that works" — and nothing is recorded. A correct one is kept and used.

This happens in the software, not in the AI, so it holds even when the AI is slow or unavailable.

5. Taking orders

A customer orders in the chat and the agent does the rest:

  1. What they want — matched to your price list only. A choice like "pink dotted or mint lined" is asked, never guessed.
  2. Where it goes — if delivery cost depends on the city, it asks the city, then the full address.
  3. How they pay — only the methods your documents name (cash on delivery, bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa…), in your own wording.
  4. The summary — items, delivery, total, address and payment, worked out from your price list.
  5. The yes — only the customer's confirmation places it. They get an order number and your delivery wording; you get a notification.

7. Being told when you are needed

Alerts reach you by email or on your phone when somebody asks for a person, when a lead is worth your attention, or when something breaks. If your WhatsApp phone goes offline, a red warning appears until it reconnects — nothing locks, and nothing is silently dropped.

8. Where it runs

One product, three ways in, all showing the same thing:

  • The desktop app, which updates itself when you open it.
  • Any web browser, at your dashboard address.
  • Your phone's browser, for checking in.

What you need to do

Setting up takes about twenty minutes, and most of it is finding files you already have. The Setup screen lists ten steps in three groups and ticks them off as you go — and the ticks are read from the real system, so a step cannot be marked done just by visiting it.

The steps can be done in any order. Start with whatever you have to hand.

Required — it cannot start without these

# Step What to have ready
1 Your business Your business name, what you sell, and your currency.
2 Prices and knowledge Your price list, catalogue or brochure. PDF, Word, Excel or photographs all work. This is what stops it inventing a price.
3 Where you are Your timezone, and the areas you cover. Every hour you state and every appointment offered is read in your zone.
4 Connect WhatsApp The phone that owns the number. You scan a QR code with it, or connect your own Twilio account.

Until all four are done, the inbox, board and analytics stay locked and PingPulse tells you which steps are still open. Test agent opens as soon as your business and prices are in, so you can try it before any customer can reach it.

WhatsApp is deliberately last: the moment it connects, real customers start getting answers.

Then: try it before anyone else does

Open Test agent and ask it what your most awkward customer would ask. It is the real agent with your real prices; nothing is sent to anyone. If an answer is wrong, the fix is almost always a document — add it under Prices and knowledge and ask again.

The rules it will not break

These are enforced in the software. They are not instructions the agent can be talked out of.

  • It never says something happened unless it did. No confirmed booking, no promised callback, unless the system actually recorded it.
  • It never invents a price. If it is not in what you uploaded, it says it will check.
  • It never promises what only you can do. It will not say it has lowered a price, changed a quotation, or will send an invoice. Asked for one, it quotes your terms.
  • It only offers pictures you have.
  • It never claims to be a person. Asked directly, it says what it is.
  • It only offers times it can actually honour.
  • It never says an appointment was booked, moved or cancelled unless the calendar changed on that message.
  • It answers each message once, and sends one follow-up once.
  • It stops when somebody asks for a person, and tells you.
  • It never messages anyone at three in the morning.
  • It never learns from its own replies — only from yours.

What it does not do

Said plainly, so there are no surprises:

  • It does not take payments. It tells customers how you take payment, in your wording, but no money moves through PingPulse.
  • It does not issue invoices, quotations or proformas.
  • It does not change your prices. It reads what you upload.
  • It does not message anyone first. It answers people who write to you.
  • It does not learn from its own replies, so a mistake does not compound.

If something goes wrong

  • A wrong answer is nearly always a missing or unclear document. Add it under Prices and knowledge and check in Test agent.
  • WhatsApp offline shows as a red warning. Reconnect the phone; nothing is lost in the meantime.
  • Anything else — take over the conversation from the inbox. The agent stops replying to that person immediately and everyone else keeps running.

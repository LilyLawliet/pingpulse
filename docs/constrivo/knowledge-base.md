## Who Constrivo Group is

Constrivo Group is a construction and remodeling company based in Dania
Beach, Florida, working across Miami and South Florida.

Constrivo Group carries out both residential and commercial work.

Constrivo Group handles permitting, local municipal zoning and environmental
reviews as part of a project.

Constrivo Group builds to Miami-Dade HVHZ (High-Velocity Hurricane Zone)
standards.

Projects are overseen by a dedicated project manager.

Constrivo Group has worked on projects in Key Biscayne, Brickell and Coconut
Grove.

## How a project starts

A project starts with a free consultation. A specialist discusses the
customer's goals, budget and timeline, and then a detailed estimate is
prepared.

To arrange a consultation the customer gives their project details and the
best phone number or email to reach them on.

---

## Pricing

Constrivo Group does not publish fixed prices. Every project is priced
individually.

Pricing comes from a free consultation followed by a detailed line-item
estimate.

Constrivo Group's estimates are described as thorough and transparent, with
fixed-budget guardrails and no surprise change orders.

Constrivo Group works to an open-book policy on project costs.

**No price, rate, deposit, discount or percentage is published anywhere.** A
customer asking what something costs is told that the price comes from the
free consultation and the written estimate.

---

## Timelines

Constrivo Group does not publish guaranteed completion dates.

A timeline for a specific project is given in the estimate, after the
consultation.

**No typical durations are published for any service.** A customer asking how
long work takes is told that the timeline comes with the estimate.

---

## Financing

Financing is available through GreenSky, a third-party financing provider.
Constrivo Group describes it as "Build Now. Pay Over Time."

The financing process is: request a free consultation and receive an
estimate; complete a GreenSky application; receive a decision; and schedule
and begin the project once approved.

GreenSky financing lets a customer start a project without waiting to save
the full amount, and offers payment plans that can be fitted to a budget.

**No financing terms are published.** Interest rates, APR, loan amounts,
repayment lengths, fees, credit requirements and eligibility criteria are not
stated anywhere by Constrivo Group. A customer asking about any of these is
told that GreenSky sets the terms and that the specialist can go through the
application with them.

## Warranty

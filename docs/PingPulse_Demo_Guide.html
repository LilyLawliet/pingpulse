Start here

Three steps, about five minutes

Everything is set up and waiting. The only thing left is telling it which WhatsApp number to answer on — that choice is yours, and you can change it whenever you like.

  1. 01

    Open your dashboard

    Go to pingpulse.duckdns.org/app in any browser and paste the access token we sent you alongside this page. That works on a Mac, a PC, a tablet — anything. On Windows there is also a desktop app, which keeps itself updated and never asks you to reinstall.

  2. 02

    Connect one WhatsApp channel

    In Settings, pick the way you want your customers to reach you.

  3. 03

    Message it from a different phone

    This part matters: send the first message from another number, not the phone you connected. A phone cannot hold a conversation with itself, so borrow a colleague's handset or use a second line.

    Watch the reply arrive on the phone and in your dashboard at the same time.

Option A · fastest

WhatsApp Web

Scan a QR code with the phone you already sell from. Your existing number, your existing chats, nothing to sign up for.

Option B

Twilio

Use your own Twilio account and business number. Better if you want the agent on a separate line from your personal one.

Connecting one disconnects the other, on purpose. Replies then always go out on one known number, so there is never a question of which number a customer is talking to.

Reading the dashboard

Every message carries its own status

You never have to wonder whether something actually reached the customer. Each reply the agent sends shows one of three states, live.

  • Sent

    It reached WhatsApp. This is the normal case.

  • Queued

    WhatsApp was briefly unreachable, so the message is held and retried automatically. It is not lost, and nothing is presented as delivered when it isn't.

  • Failed

    It could not be delivered and will not be retried — for example a number that WhatsApp refuses. Rare, and always visible rather than silent.

Since the last time you saw it

What we improved

  • Nothing gets lost

    If WhatsApp drops for a few seconds, replies queue and go out by themselves instead of disappearing.

  • Messages reach the right chat

    We fixed an addressing fault where a reply looked sent on our side but never arrived on the customer's phone.

  • The dashboard keeps up

    Replies, follow-ups and delivery states all appear the moment they happen, without refreshing.

  • Scheduled follow-ups

    Nudge a quiet conversation in your own words, at a time you choose — and it cancels if they write back.

  • Works on a Mac

    The full dashboard now runs in a browser, identical to the desktop app rather than a cut-down version.

  • Updates arrive on their own

    The desktop app updates itself on launch. No reinstalling, no downloading anything twice.

  • One number, no ambiguity

    Connecting a WhatsApp method disconnects the other, so replies always go out on the number you chose.

  • Your workspace is yours

    Your conversations and catalogue are separate from every other account on the system, enforced at the server.
